    Picked in Flores

    Roasted in our shop

    Every Monday we roast in small batches to keep the aroma and flavor fresh.

  3. 03

    Brewed for you

    Our baristas weigh and brew every cup. Espresso or pour-over, the choice is yours.

FAQ

Questions we often get

Do you have Wi-Fi and power outlets?

Yes. Wi-Fi is free for every guest, and every table has a power outlet.

Can I book a space for an event?

Yes. The upstairs room fits about 25 people and is often used for discussions, club meetings, or campus events. Please book at least one day ahead.

Do you have caffeine-free or dairy-free options?

Yes. Try the Lemongrass Ginger Tea or the Iced Lime & Honey. Any milk drink can also be made with oat milk.

How can I pay?

Cash, QRIS, and debit cards.
